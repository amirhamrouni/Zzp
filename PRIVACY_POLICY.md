# Privacy Policy — ZZP BTW Tracker

Last updated: 3 September 2026

ZZP BTW Tracker is a local-first bookkeeping application. It does not require an account and does not transmit bookkeeping records to a developer-operated server.

The app can store transactions, customers, invoices, company details, working hours, trips, receipt text and document references in its local database. Camera and selected images are processed only when the user starts receipt recognition; OCR runs on the device.

PDF and CSV files are created only after a user action. A selected Android destination or sharing app receives an exported file under its own privacy terms.

Users can delete records inside the app. Uninstalling removes the local database, subject to Android backup settings. Files explicitly exported outside the app must be deleted separately.

The app blocks clear-text network traffic and uses scoped document permissions and Android FileProvider. Before public release, the publisher must add a support email and public privacy-policy URL here and in Google Play.
